docs: the v0.4.2 handover, the reliability documents, and the CI additions

The documentation is brought in line with what the code now does, and the
duplicates from the previous round are removed.

NEW:

- docs/reliability/BURST-CAPTURE-ROOT-CAUSE.md
  The incident, the two root causes, the measured before/after, the platform
  limit that is not ours to fix, and the remaining risks.
- docs/reliability/QUEUE-BACKPRESSURE.md
  The queue design, the measured depth and wait percentiles, what happens on
  overflow, and the honest statement that the in-memory queue loses events if
  the process dies.
- docs/reliability/NOTIFICATION-DEDUP.md
  The dedup key, the three versions of the fallback and why each failed, the
  eight boundary cases, and the 25-message platform limit.
- docs/reliability/X200PRO-ORIGINOS-MATRIX.md
  What to collect on the device, the three vivo background settings that have
  to be enabled together, the four denominators to record, and the layered
  diagnosis that separates a system delivery gap from an app failure. Every
  OriginOS row is marked NOT_TESTED, because no device was attached.
- docs/roadmap/MULTI-DEVICE-SYNC.md
  The planning only. It states plainly that the offline connector and a future
  automatic sync are different things, and that the offline connector has no
  network at all.

The notification mirror, Rust core and media documents are updated rather
than duplicated, and the stale copies from the previous round are removed.

CI gains the DHC cross-language fixture generation and a contract
compatibility check, so a change to the contract on one side without the
other fails the build.
